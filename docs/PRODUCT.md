# Product statement

## Client
University students and university instructors at a single university,
communicating about coursework. Reached through our own experience as
students at this university using Moodle. Universities first; a later
schools phase would add child-privacy and parental-consent requirements, not
covered now.

## The process today
A student has a question about a course or an assignment. They post it in a
Moodle message/forum or send an email to the instructor. The instructor does
not have Moodle or email open continuously, so the message sits unread. On
average it takes around 12 hours for the student to get a reply, so they
often don't get an answer before they need it (e.g. before working on the
assignment or before an exam).

## Baseline metric
~12 hours average time for a student to get a reply to a course question via
Moodle or email today. Source: team-estimated from our own experience as
students at this university; to be backed by a short student survey if time
allows.

## The statement
When a university student is waiting on an instructor's answer to a course
question but replies on Moodle or email take around 12 hours, our
AI-assisted communication channel summarizes long questions, converts
between text and voice so either side can reply however is easiest, and lets
the instructor see and answer without opening email or logging into Moodle,
so that average response time goes from 12 hours to 2 hours.

## Out of scope
1. Voice or video calls — students and instructors keep using other
   platforms for that. The app never carries a live call; it only supports
   AI-generated voice memos (text-to-speech and speech-to-text), and raw
   audio is never sent between users.
2. Grading, gradebook, and assignment submission.
3. Course content and file hosting (materials repository).

Constraints: open-source AI models/tools only for now (provider and cost
undecided); interface and core tasks must work in English and Estonian.
